Zifeng Wen (文梓锋).

Undergraduate thesis at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis Award, School of Computer Science and Technology (16 recipients in the Class of 2026).

English title: *Minimized Design of Clec11a Protein Based on Diffusion Models*.

Clec11a is a secreted protein whose functional core is of interest for compact, designable variants. This thesis studies how diffusion-based generative models can be used for *minimized protein design*: producing shorter sequences and structures that still keep the features associated with Clec11a function. The work frames the design objective, builds a generation-and-evaluation pipeline around diffusion models, and examines whether the resulting variants remain structurally plausible after sequence compression.

哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）本科毕业论文。围绕 Clec11a 的最小化设计问题，用扩散模型生成更紧凑的蛋白变体，并在压缩序列与结构规模的同时尽量保留功能相关特征。获计算机学院优秀毕业论文。
