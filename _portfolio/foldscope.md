Research Assistant, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, Mar. 2025 – Jul. 2025. Supervisor: Prof. Junjie Chen.

Built an immersive protein design and validation platform that connects diffusion-based generation with structure–function evaluation in VR.

- Python backend bridging a C# VR client with PDB structures and RFdiffusion for constrained backbone generation
- Integrated ProteinMPNN for sequence design and RF3 for refolding, with online RMSD and pTM checks
- Outcomes: 2 Chinese national invention patent applications and 1 software copyright registration
